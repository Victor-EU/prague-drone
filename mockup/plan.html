WHAT THE SET TEACHES THE BUILD

ROOFSCAPE & MASSINGPetřín and Vyšehrad panoramas, Letná bridge views. What the drone sees most: roof forms, dormer and chimney density, the tile / slate / copper mix, how streets cut the blocks.
LANDMARK SHAPESCharles Bridge and its towers, Týn, St Nicholas, the Castle, the Dancing House, Vyšehrad spires, the water tower. Modelled to be recognisable from any angle.
MATERIALS & COLOURMalá Strana streets, embankment facades, bridge stone, cobbles, window boxes. The palette every generic block is painted with.
LIGHT & SKYWarm clear morning, cumulus midday, overcast pastel evening, blue hour, night. The four ends of the time-of-day slider, plus cloud shapes and coverage.
THE RIVER & LIFEWater colour by light, weir foam, pedal boats, tour boats, rowers, swans, pigeons, red trams, tourists on the bridge.
THE SEASONRose garden, orchards, mock orange, unripe plums and cherries, water lilies, wet leaves, big lawn trees. Early summer, not high summer.
